# NNNN. Title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN (link the newer ADR)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** names or teams

## Context

The forces at play: the problem, constraints, requirements, what is true today.

## Options

1. **Option A:** what it is; pros; cons.
2. **Option B:** what it is; pros; cons.

## Decision

The option chosen and the reason, in a few sentences.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what must be done now (migrations, docs, follow-up
work), and how we would know the decision was wrong.
